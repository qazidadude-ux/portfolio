Most "handoff" processes are really just a designer throwing a Figma file over a wall and hoping for the best. The best teams I've worked with don't hand off — they collaborate the whole way through.

## Involve engineering before the first pixel

Bring your engineers into early exploration, not just final review. They'll catch feasibility problems while a change is still a five-minute conversation instead of a two-day rebuild.

## Build a shared vocabulary

A design system isn't just for designers. When component names, spacing tokens, and states are shared language between design and code, "translation loss" mostly disappears.

## Ship small, together

Instead of one big reveal, ship in small increments and look at real, running software together. Feedback gets sharper and faster when everyone's looking at the same thing.
