---
title: Why faster isn't always better
date: 2025-04-22
author: Joseph Alexander
excerpt: Speed is a seductive metric, but shipping fast without shipping right just moves the cost of quality further down the road.
---

There's a version of "move fast" that means "think clearly, then act decisively." There's another version that means "skip the thinking." Only one of these is actually fast in the long run.

## The real cost of rushed design

A rushed interface doesn't disappear — it becomes technical and design debt that someone (often you) pays for later, usually with interest.

## Fast decisions still need good inputs

Speed comes from clarity, not from skipping steps. A tight, well-scoped brief lets you move quickly with confidence instead of quickly with anxiety.

## Where to actually cut time

Cut research you don't need, meetings that don't change decisions, and polish on things nobody will see. Don't cut the five minutes it takes to sanity-check an idea before you build on top of it.
